Logline: In a time of devastating plagues and ancient superstitions, a brilliant physician defies conventional wisdom to meticulously document, innovate, and establish the foundational principles of Traditional Chinese Medicine, saving countless lives and leaving an enduring legacy.

Three-Act Plot Outline:

Act 1: The Shadow of Sickness
The Eastern Han Dynasty is in decline, plagued by political instability and, more devastatingly, widespread epidemics, referred to as "cold damage diseases." Young Zhang Zhongjing, a compassionate and observant physician, witnesses firsthand the suffering and death, including within his own family. He is frustrated by the limitations of existing medical practices, which often rely on superstition or ineffective remedies. Driven by a deep desire to heal, he begins to question traditional approaches and embarks on a personal quest to understand the true nature of illness and find more effective treatments, often facing skepticism and resistance from established, conservative practitioners.

Act 2: The Crucible of Discovery
Zhang Zhongjing dedicates himself to rigorous study, meticulous observation, and tireless experimentation. He travels, gathers knowledge, and synthesizes existing medicinal experience, all while documenting symptoms, disease progression, and treatment outcomes with unprecedented detail. He faces numerous setbacks, failures, and personal sacrifices, including long hours, isolation, and the emotional toll of losing patients. Through perseverance, he begins to formulate his revolutionary principles of differential diagnosis and tailored treatments, laying the groundwork for his seminal work, the "Shanghan Lun." A particularly challenging outbreak or a personal crisis pushes him to a critical breakthrough, solidifying his new medical philosophy.

Act 3: A Legacy Forged in Healing
Zhang Zhongjing completes the "Shanghan Lun" (Treatise on Cold Damage Diseases), a comprehensive and systematic medical text that revolutionizes the understanding and treatment of diseases. His methods, initially met with resistance, prove undeniably effective in combating the widespread epidemics, earning him growing recognition and respect. He establishes a school or trains a dedicated group of disciples, ensuring his invaluable knowledge is preserved and disseminated. The film concludes by showcasing the profound and lasting impact of his work, illustrating how his principles continue to save lives and shape Traditional Chinese Medicine for centuries to come, cementing his status as one of history's greatest medical pioneers.
